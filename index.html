About Our Trust

A Ray of Hope for the Underprivileged

Aachi Educational and Charitable Trust was established in 2025 in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, with a noble vision to create a brighter future for the marginalized sections of society. Our trust is legally registered under the Indian Trust Act and has been working tirelessly to bring positive change.

Founded by a group of passionate social workers, Aachi Trust focuses on four key pillars — Education, Healthcare, Food Security, and Empowerment. We believe that every child deserves access to quality education, every family deserves proper healthcare, and no one should go to bed hungry. Our programs are designed to address these fundamental needs while promoting self-reliance and dignity among the communities we serve.

What We Do

Our Core Services

We work across multiple verticals to ensure holistic development and support for the underprivileged communities.

Education Support

Free school supplies, tuition fees, after-school coaching centers, scholarship programs, and digital literacy classes for underprivileged students from Class 1 to college level.

Medical Assistance

Financial aid for surgeries, hospital bills, medications, monthly free medical camps, blood donation drives, and health awareness programs in rural and urban slum areas.

Food Security

Daily mid-day meals, weekend food packets, community kitchens during festivals, ration kit distribution, and nutrition programs for malnourished children and pregnant women.

Women Empowerment

Self-help group formation, vocational training in tailoring, embroidery, and computer skills, micro-finance support, and awareness programs on rights and hygiene.

Child Welfare

Child rights protection, orphanage support, child labor rescue and rehabilitation, counseling services, recreational activities, and birthday celebrations for orphan children.

Old Age Support

Care and support for abandoned elderly, monthly pension assistance, medical check-ups, food and clothing distribution, and emotional support through regular visits and companionship programs.
